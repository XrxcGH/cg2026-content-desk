Shooting notes

The white-backdrop trap: most FRC robots are bare aluminium and white polycarbonate, which is exactly what an automated background remover deletes. Add a rim light from behind, and expose the backdrop about a stop brighter than the robot but not blown to pure white — around 235–245 still cuts cleanly and keeps the edges. Light grey works even better than white.

Consistency beats quality: same three-quarter angle, camera at robot mid-height, signature pose (arm up, intake out), full robot with a little headroom. Six matched photos look better than three great ones and three snaps.

Check every cutout against a dark background before trusting it — the thumbnails below render on the same purple the broadcast uses, which is where fringing and holes show up.
